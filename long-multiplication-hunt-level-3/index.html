🎯 What's new?
Before: 2-digit × 2-digit (like 56 × 34).
Now: 3-digit × 2-digit (like 234 × 17).

Same recipe — just one more digit on top. Same two rows (× ones, then × tens with placeholder 0). Same final addition.

Bigger numbers = more carries to track. Squared paper is your friend. ✏️

⭐ Worked Example — 234 × 17 = ?

Estimate first: 234 ≈ 200, 17 ≈ 20. So 200 × 20 = 4000. Real answer near 4000.

Notice: with 3-digit numbers, you get MORE carries. Write them small + clear, in the right column. Don't lose them.
