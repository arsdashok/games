01
Speech Detective
Your new case · 2 min
🕵️🔍
Detective Detective, your mission: every sentence is one of THREE types. Detectives never guess. Detectives run the test.
🗨️
DIRECT
The EXACT words someone says.
Petey said I am a genius.
📖
REPORTED
Someone TELLS us what was said.
Petey said that he was a genius.
🐾
REGULAR
Nobody talks. Something just happens.
Petey built a giant robot.
😼 Same cat, three types of sentence. Your detective tool is on the next card: the 2-Step Test.

The 2-Step Detective Test

🔍 Watch the test work

Chief said I need a giant cup of coffee.
Step 1: talking word? said ✓
Step 2: words after it: "I need a giant cup of coffee."
Bubble voice: Chief CAN say that. It fits! 🗨️
DIRECT
One-step case: Dog Man chewed the sofa.
Talking word? None.  REGULAR  Done in one step!
⚠️ Detective warning: do NOT hunt for inverted commas. Sneaky sentences hide them. Trust the 2 steps.
03
One story, three ways
Watch the bubble · 5 min
🍦
Same ice cream, three different sentences. The bubble tells you which is which.
Li'l Petey said I want ice cream.
DIRECT
The exact words fit the bubble. ✔
Li'l Petey said that he wanted ice cream.
REPORTED
He would never call himself "he"! The bubble breaks. You are telling the story.
Li'l Petey ate his ice cream.
REGULAR
No talking word at all. One step, done.
