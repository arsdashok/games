Game 1
~8 min
Two Truths and a Lie
Rules: Say 3 things about yourself. Two are true, one is a lie. The other person guesses which one is the lie.
Then swap!
Teacher goes first to show how it works and reduce pressure.

Teacher's example

2.
3.

TRUE
LIE

Player's turn

2.
3.

LIE

Game 3
~5 min
Picture Talk — Describe & Discuss
Rules: Look at each picture/topic. Describe what you see. Then answer the question.
Any level works — A1 says a few words, B2 tells a story.
👨‍👩‍👧
Family
Tell me about your family.
Who do you live with?
What do you like to do together?
🏠
Home
Describe your home.
What's your favourite room?
Do you like living in Abu Dhabi?
✈️
Travel
Do you like travelling?
Where was your last trip?
Where would you like to go?
💼
Work / Daily life
What do you do?
What does a typical day look like?
What do you enjoy most?
🎯
Goals
Why are you learning English?
What's your goal for this year?
How will you use English?
🎉
Free time
What do you do for fun?
Do you have any hobbies?
What did you do last weekend?
💡 Используй как запасной вариант если останется время, или пропусти если уже достаточно наговорились.
