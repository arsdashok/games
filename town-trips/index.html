Microphone help

Press Speak and allow microphone access. Take your time: pauses are fine. Press Go when your sentence is ready. Stop keeps your words without starting a trip. You can also type or edit the sentence. The microphone stops when you leave or hide the page. Your browser may send audio to its speech recognition service. This game does not save recordings or sentences.
