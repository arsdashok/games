Teacher notes

Step 1 only (Dasha, 23 Oct): the pronoun from the picture, no verb. 12 pictures a round: 4 he, 4 she, 4 they, new order every time. The noun (a witch, two ghosts) appears after the answer, as a bonus word.

Start: she says the pronoun aloud, then you or she clicks it. A wrong first try gives a hint and no sweet; she tries again.

Fast round: the same, timed; the best time is kept on this computer and counts only with 10 or more sweets. Run it after one normal round.

My turn to answer: roles swapped. You say a pronoun (sometimes wrong on purpose), she says «right» or «wrong»; if it is wrong, she says the right one. Then click «Show».

Check (the hinge): three pictures, no buttons, no help: one boy, a boy and a girl, one woman. She names all three; then «Show».

Mixed pairs (a witch and a vampire) are deliberately included: two people = they, not «she and he».

Move to step 2 (he plays / they play, two verbs) only when a round goes through with almost no hints.
