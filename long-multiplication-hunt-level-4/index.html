📖 The Multi-Step Reading Trick

🎯 When a problem has TWO parts
Read the WHOLE story first. Then look for the signal words:
  • "each" / "per" → likely multiplication (one group × number of groups)
  • "then he..." / "after that..." → this signals Step 2 uses the answer from Step 1
  • "how many more" / "how many left" → subtraction at the end
  • "total" / "altogether" → addition at the end
🪜 The Two-Box Trick
Don't try to solve it all in your head. Use two boxes on paper:
Step 1 box — what's the first calculation? Do it.
Step 2 box — use Step 1's answer to do the next calculation.
Done!

⭐ Worked Example — Steve's Diamond Trade

Steve mines diamonds. He gets 16 diamonds per day and mines for 12 days. Then he sells each diamond for 3 emeralds. How many emeralds does Steve get altogether?

Signal words spotted: "per day" + "each diamond" + "altogether" → multiplication then multiplication.

Step 1 — diamonds mined
16 per day × 12 days = ?
16 × 12 = 192 diamonds
Step 2 — emeralds from trade
192 diamonds × 3 emeralds = ?
192 × 3 = 576 emeralds
✨ Final answer: 576 emeralds

Notice: the answer to Step 1 (192) became the starting number for Step 2. That's the chain.
