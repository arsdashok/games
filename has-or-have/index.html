for the teacher
On every card she SAYS each step before the tap (you can tap for her while sharing the screen): who (he / she / it / they), then the question with Has or Have, then the short answer. Listen for they + have / haven't: a spoken «she have» is a miss even if the tap is right.
On a wrong tap the page shows the chain (X1 and X2 → they → have): say the whole question and answer once and move on, do not re-explain.
Cards 13-16 have no buttons: she says the whole chain, the question and the answer, you press Check and mark it by ear.
Pronouns are the comic's, as used on 20 Oct: Wonder Cat she, Superdog he (Doug in his suit), Wonder Wheels he, X1 / X2 it (a robot, your rule), any two together they. A he / it slip on a single robot is not the plural error; the miss list shows what she chose.
The end screen gives cards right first time; «Play the missed ones» replays only those.
