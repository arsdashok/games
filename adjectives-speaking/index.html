✏️ Games

Game 1 — Show Me! 🎭 TPR · 3 min

Teacher says a word → Player shows it with her body / face!

Then swap — Player shows, teacher guesses.

"Happy!" → 😊 big smile
"Sad!" → 😢 sad face
"Big!" → 🙆 arms wide
"Small!" → 🤏 fingers close together
"Old!" → 🧓 walk slowly
"Long!" → 📏 stretch arms
"Dirty!" → 🤢 "ewww" face

💡 No Russian needed — it's all body language! Speed up for fun.
Game 2 — Yes or No? 👍👎 Speaking · 4 min

Teacher says a sentence. Player says "Yes!" or "No!" (+ corrects if no).

🐘 An elephant is small.
🌞 The sun is big.
👶 A baby is old.
🐍 A snake is short.
🌹 A flower is beautiful.
🐷 A pig is clean.
🤡 A clown is sad.
🦒 A giraffe is long.

Example:
T: "An elephant is small." → K: "No! An elephant is big!"
T: "The sun is big." → K: "Yes!"

💡 If she says "No" — she has to say the correct word. Keep it fast and silly!
Answers: 1. No — big! 2. Yes! 3. No — young! 4. No — long! 5. Yes! 6. No — dirty! 7. No — happy! 8. Yes!

Game 4 — Point and Say! 👉 Speaking · 4 min

Look at your room / screen. Teacher points at things → Player describes with an adjective.

Then Player points — teacher describes!

Examples:
👉 (pen) → "It's small!"
👉 (table) → "It's big!"
👉 (hands after painting) → "They're dirty!"
👉 (mum's necklace) → "It's beautiful!"
👉 (hair) → "It's long!"

💡 Accept anything correct! "The pencil is long" and "The pencil is small" are both fine.
